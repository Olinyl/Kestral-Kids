# Case brief and team notes used by this project

Sources supplied by the owner: the Kestrel Kids team Google Doc linked in SPEC.md and Pending Chaos at Kestrel Kids (Case).pdf. This summary travels with the repository so a cloud worker does not need the owner's Downloads folder.

## Instructor phone workflow

- An instructor teaches at most six children and carries a radio. Personal phone batteries can fail in the cold; internet access cannot be assumed.
- Show the assigned group's children, bibs, ability level and the medical/allergy information needed for that group. Do not expose all children's medical files or general family contact lists.
- Provide a clear radio handoff for a missing child: stable child identity, last seen time and place, confirmed clothing/equipment, possible injuries and relevant medical alert.
- For Patrol, reconcile the registration and consent names under one child ID, show uncertainty instead of presenting an instructor's incorrect clothing description as fact, and clearly mark a lift scan unavailable when the pass number is unknown. Include visibility, relevant medical detail, emergency contacts for police handoff, and the case's police/SAR escalation timing.
- Patrol conducts searches. Police and family escalation belongs to patrol's established procedure, not an automated instructor notification.
- Offline access is a stretch goal; the preview must not imply it works offline or that an unsent report reached patrol.

## Front desk desktop workflow

- Check in many children quickly by name, group or bib and record current clothing rather than relying on memory.
- Show missing consent or emergency information clearly. The owner confirmed that missing guardian consent blocks check-in, with no staff override.
- Show both emergency contacts, medical alerts, linked guardian and currently authorized pickup adults.
- Pickup authorization can be temporary and must be evaluated for the lesson day. A claimed family relationship does not authorize pickup.
- Record actual release adult, verifying staff and time. An unauthorized adult cannot complete release.
- Log instructor radio reports with last seen details, equipment, clothing and injuries so patrol has one consistent record. Do not invent lift-scan access or send family messages automatically.

## Shared boundaries

Registration and payments stay in the resort store. Instructor scheduling/pay and progress assessments are out of scope. Store spreadsheet import, bulk parent updates and offline functionality are stretch goals.

One child ID ties together different surnames in registrations and consent forms. Each profile has two emergency contacts, recorded medical information, guardian-provided consent status and authorized pickup adults. All demo data is fictional. Preview role switching is not access control.
