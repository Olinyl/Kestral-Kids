# Describe the app before building

1. Start from what they already told you. Propose a full first draft, then ask
   only about what is missing or wrong, one question at a time.
2. First question: "Do you want a quick first version to try out, or a complete
   version ready to hand over?"
3. Cover: who uses it, what they can do, what records it keeps, who can see
   what, and any emails or reminders it sends. Use their words, not technical
   ones.
4. Do not ask them to read a long document. Show a card:
   - the app's name and one sentence on what it is for
   - at most five things people can do with it
   - your guesses (at most three) as short questions, e.g. "I assumed members
     only see their own bookings. Right?"
5. Ask "Want to see it?" and wait. Their answer is their decision; never assume
   it.
6. Write no app code while describing. The rules for building come from
   `piezas_contract`.
